THE WORLD OF MOZake

STORY

EVERY
CREATION
HAS A
BEGINNING.

MOZake began with a simple idea: to create something that didn't need to fit in.

A character without a mouth. Without a perfect shape. Without a place it was supposed to belong.

MOZake became a reflection of the outsider — the odd one out, the unfinished idea, the thing that doesn't quite make sense until you give it a place to exist.

MOZake discovers colour, expression and the ability to leave something behind.
